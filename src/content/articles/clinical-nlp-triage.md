abstract: >-
  Emergency departments generate unstructured clinical notes faster than staff
  can review them. We describe a privacy-preserving NLP system that runs entirely
  on-premise to prioritise notes by acuity, using de-identification and federated
  fine-tuning to keep patient data local. Deployed across two hospital networks,
  the system surfaced high-acuity cases a median of 22 minutes earlier while
  meeting institutional data-governance requirements, illustrating responsible
  applied AI in healthcare.
