abstract: >-
  Battery-constrained edge devices must balance responsiveness against energy
  budgets. This paper introduces an adaptive scheduler that predicts workload
  bursts and shifts non-critical inference to low-power windows. In a smart-
  building pilot spanning 240 sensor nodes, the scheduler extended median device
  uptime by 2.3x without violating latency service-level objectives, showing how
  applied scheduling policy can materially reduce operational cost at the edge.
